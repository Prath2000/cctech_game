## Seating props (TASK-1.7, seating half)
- All seating geometry (PUSH vending machine, Voltas-style coolers, chairs, tables, glasses, snack packets, bins, ceiling fans) is modelled procedurally in `blender/props/seating.py`; no third-party models. Brand names are text only, for a faithful private reproduction.
- `assets/decals/seating/mural_circuit_arrow.jpg`: cropped from the project's own reference photo (references/img/photos, `15.25.08`); no third-party licence.

## Pantry props (TASK-1.7, pantry half)
- All pantry meshes (LG fridge/microwave, Tata Bistro machine, kettle, sink, faucet, cabinets, mugs, flasks, jug, switch plate, LED panels, ceiling fan) are modelled procedurally in `blender/props/pantry.py` from product dimensions; no third-party model files used (Poly Haven models from asset-sources.md were not needed).
- Brand decals in `assets/decals/pantry/` (Tata Bistro screen, LG logos, LG fridge stickers) are crops of the project's own reference photos (`references/img/photos/`). Trademarks (TATA, LG) belong to their owners; used only as in-room set dressing for a non-commercial internal prototype.
